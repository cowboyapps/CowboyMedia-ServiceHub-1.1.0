---
name: ServiceHub account separation
description: Customer-facing distinction between ServiceHub and payment/service portal accounts.
---

ServiceHub is not the same account customers use to access their payment and service information online. Customers need to create a new account for ServiceHub.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this distinction on the sign-in page so customers do not try to sign in with their payment/service portal account.

**How to apply:** Keep this distinction clear in onboarding and sign-in wording. Do not imply that existing payment/service portal credentials automatically sign customers into ServiceHub.
